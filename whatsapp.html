💬 EAS Parent WhatsApp Community

Parent-run. Privacy-first. ParentSquare is the source of truth for official school information.

The goal: A parent community where people feel comfortable connecting, asking questions, and supporting each other.

What is this group?

The EAS Parent WhatsApp Community is a parent-run space for families at the Environmental & Adventure School to coordinate, connect, and build community. It is not affiliated with LWSD or EAS, and no faculty or staff are in the group.

For official updates (schedules, assignments, closures, policies), always check ParentSquare. If a question is something already addressed by the school, we should and will include the ParentSquare link. We won't rewrite official school communication. In addition, all communication in the group will follow the EAS Communication Policy, which emphasizes communication, trust, and respect with and for teachers.

What the group is for

  • Quick coordination: carpools, gear swaps, ride shares, shuttle updates
  • Asking questions and connecting with other parents
  • General conversation and community building

What the group is not for

  • Official school communication (that stays on ParentSquare)
  • Teacher or staff complaints (use the EAS Communication Policy and go directly to teachers)
  • Sharing class lists, student records, or sensitive student information

Photo policy

While FERPA (the federal student privacy law) does not technically apply to a private, parent-run group, we hold ourselves to the same privacy principles as the district.

No student photos posted directly in the WhatsApp group.
Instead, upload photos to the PTO Yearbook OneDrive folders. The photos still get shared, they just live somewhere more appropriate. This keeps things simple: no consent lists to maintain, no moderation headaches, and no one is put in the position of policing what parents post.

Managing your student's privacy at the district level

If you want to opt out of directory information sharing at the district level (including photos on the school website, Instagram, yearbook, etc.), you can do so through Skyward Family Access:

How to opt out: Log into Skyward Family Access → click Student Information on the left → click Request Changes in the top right.

You can also write to Principal Dahl directly. This must be done before September 14 each year.

Full details: LWSD FERPA Policy

Moderation

The group is managed by 2-3 parent admins, not just one person. Admin roles rotate annually and are open to volunteers. Interested in helping out? Email president@easpto.org.

Common questions

Do I have to join?
Nope. The WhatsApp group is completely optional. All official school communication goes through ParentSquare, so you'll never miss anything important.
Can I share photos from school events?
Not directly in WhatsApp. Upload them to the PTO Yearbook OneDrive folders instead, following the same privacy principles the district uses.
What if the group is sending me too many notifications?
Mute the chat! You can mute any chat that's too active for you and check in when you feel like it. This is like any other social media platform. Parents are free to talk, and if the volume isn't for you, WhatsApp gives you full control over your notifications right in the app.
What if someone is being disrespectful?
Admins will address it. We keep things kind and practical. If there's an ongoing issue, reach out to the admins directly.
What if I have a concern about a teacher or assignment?
Please reach out to the teacher directly, following the EAS Communication Policy. The WhatsApp group is not the place for teacher or staff complaints.
How do I leave the group?
You can leave any chat at any time through WhatsApp. No questions asked.
