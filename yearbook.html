Yearbook photos

Help us build this year's yearbook. Adding your photos takes just a few taps, no account or login needed.

Our yearbook is only as good as the photos families share. If you snapped pictures at a field trip, an elective, spirit week, a social, or any EAS moment, we would love to have them. It is quick, it is free, and you do not need to sign in to anything.

Tap the button below, pick the folder that matches your photos, and upload. That's it.

📸 Upload your photos

Opens our photo library in a new tab. Choose a folder, then add your photos.

Before you upload

Pick the right folder. Photos are sorted into folders like "Excursions - Bowman Bay" or "Extracurricular - Spirit Week." Open the folder that matches your photos before you upload so they land in the right spot.

Send full-size photos. Please upload the original, full-size images. When your phone asks, choose "Actual size" or "Original," not a smaller version. Yearbook photos get printed, and small or shrunk images can look blurry on the page.

Add as many as you like. There is no limit. More photos means a better yearbook, so do not hold back.

How to upload, step by step

📱 On your phone

  • 1Tap Upload your photos above. The photo library opens in your browser.
  • 2Tap the folder that matches your photos (for example, "Excursions - Camp Hamilton").
  • 3Tap + Add or the Upload button near the top of the screen.
  • 4Choose Photo library or Files, then select the photos you want to add.
  • 5If asked about size, pick Actual size / Original. Then tap Upload or Done.

💻 On a computer

  • 1Click Upload your photos above. The photo library opens in a new tab.
  • 2Double-click the folder that matches your photos to open it.
  • 3Drag your photos from your computer straight into the folder, or click Upload at the top.
  • 4Wait for the upload to finish. You'll see your photos appear in the folder.

The folders you'll see

Photos are grouped by category so they are easy to find. Pick whichever fits best. If you are not sure, "Extracurricular - Daily Life" or "Whole School - All-School" are safe catch-alls.
